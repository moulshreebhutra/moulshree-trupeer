Trupeer feature proposal

Product updates silently break Trupeer guides. This is the problem Trupeer should solve next.

Trupeer turns one screen recording into a polished video and a step-by-step doc, in 120+ languages. But the software inside those guides ships every sprint. A button is renamed, a step moves, and the guide quietly becomes wrong. Nobody knows which guides broke until a customer gets stuck, and the usual fix is a full re-record.

Why now: Trupeer guides no longer just play as videos. They power Ask Trupeer and reach Claude and ChatGPT through MCP, so one outdated step becomes a wrong answer everywhere an AI agent reads it.

01 · The insight

Trupeer is becoming the source of truth for how software works.

This year Trupeer shipped knowledge bases on a company's own domain, Ask Trupeer, an MCP server and Governance with approvals and audit logs. It's becoming the place people and AI agents go to learn how a product works.

A source of truth has one job: to be true.

But the software inside every guide changes every sprint. That's the gap this proposal closes.

03 · The solution

Living Guides: guides that know when they're wrong.

One layer across the whole library that turns a silent, months-long problem into a five-minute task.

1

Detect

Pinpoints the exact step that no longer matches the live product, building on what Trupeer already has: click detection and viewer analytics.

2

Fix

Recommends the cheapest fix: update the words, re-record one step, or dismiss. Credit cost shown upfront.

3

Sync

One approval updates the video, the doc, every translation and Ask Trupeer's answers.

Every workspace also gets a freshness score: one number that tells an admin, on renewal day, that their library is current.

04 · How it works

Built on signals Trupeer already has

No new crawler and no extra work for creators in v1.

Signal 1

Re-check on the next visit

The recorder already detects every click. Also save the label and page of each clicked element, then compare them when the creator next visits that page.

Signal 2

Viewers raise a hand

A one-tap "this looks different" on every step, plus drop-off spikes at a single timestamp.

Signal 3

Release notes

Paste release notes or connect Linear or Jira; changed feature names are matched to scripts and on-screen text.

What changedRecommended fixCost
A label or a few wordsUpdate the text and screenshot, re-voice one line, update it in every languageOne step
A step was added or movedRe-record just that step and swap it in with MergeOne clip
Colour or spacing onlyDismiss, and the detector learns from itFree
The whole flow was redesignedRe-record the guide, now the rare exceptionFull video

06 · The rollout

Four weekly releases, each one usable on its own

Week 1

Viewer flags + freshness score

Live with 5 design partners, human signals only.

Week 2

Step-level detection

Re-check on the next visit; precision checked on 100 flags.

Week 3

One-approval fixes

Synced to docs and translations.

Week 4

Agent freshness

Warnings in Ask Trupeer and MCP, plus release triggers.

Quick wins alongside
  • Credit preview before Generate, Translate or Refresh Voiceover
  • Renewal reminders and a receipt for every charge
  • A 30-day trash; deleted videos can't be recovered today
Risks, and how I'd handle them
  • False alarms from A/B tests: require two signals to auto-flag
  • Fewer re-records, fewer credits: offset by retention and Enterprise
  • Flags nobody owns: creator owns by default, admins reassign

My 90-day plan

Listen, prove, scale.

Living Guides is my strongest hypothesis, not a fixed plan. The first 30 days decide whether it's the right bet.

30Listen

Learn the customers and the numbers

  • Talk to 20+ customers; sit in on sales and support calls.
  • Turn reviews, tickets and churn notes into one ranked problem map.
  • Set up Mixpanel or Amplitude funnels from record to share to view.
  • Ship 2 quick wins: credit preview and renewal reminders.

Outcome: baselines and a problem map the founders agree on.

60Prove

Validate and ship v1

  • Prototype in Figma and with AI tools; test with 5 design partners.
  • Ship in weekly slices, starting with viewer flags and the score.
  • Own one activation metric and run 2 experiments on it.
  • Set the rhythm: one-page PRDs, weekly release notes, Friday metrics.

Outcome: Living Guides live with design partners, and a first activation lift.

90Scale

Show the impact, plan what's next

  • Ship one-approval fixes and agent freshness.
  • Roll out to every paid workspace; package extras for Enterprise.
  • Report time to fix, fixes without re-records and credits per fix.
  • Propose next quarter's roadmap, with a clear "not now" list.

Outcome: a feature customers use every week, with numbers to prove it.
